9 GRID Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

9 GRID (「三國 3×3 card battle」) is a single-player iOS game. This page is the privacy policy for the App Store listing.

What we collect

There is no advertising, no analytics SDK, and no tracking. The game has no server of its own.

If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple gives this app a stable user id, and on the first sign-in may also give a name and an email (which may be a private relay address). Those values stay in the save file on this device. They are not uploaded.

Progress (owned cards, deck, copper, jade) is stored only on your device. Guest play does not use an Apple id.

我們收集什麼

沒有廣告、分析工具或跨 App 追蹤。遊戲沒有自己的伺服器。

若你選擇透過 Apple 登入，Apple 會給這款 App 一個固定使用者編號；第一次登入時也可能提供姓名與電郵（可以是隱藏電郵）。這些只寫在這台裝置的存檔裡，不會上傳。

進度（持有卡、牌組、銅錢、玉）只存在你的裝置上。訪客入營不會使用 Apple 編號。
